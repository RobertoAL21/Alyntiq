# Alyntiq

AI-powered quantitative research and paper trading platform for building, backtesting, deploying, and evaluating intelligent trading strategies using real market data.

## Project Status

🚧 Under active development.

## Vision

Alyntiq is an experimental quantitative research platform designed to combine:

- Machine Learning
- Quantitative Finance
- Backtesting
- Risk Management
- Real-Time Market Data
- Paper Trading
- MLOps
- AI Engineering

The platform is designed for research and educational purposes and does not execute trades with real money.

## Core Architecture

Market Data → Features → Models → Strategy → Risk → Execution → Portfolio

## Environments

- Research
- Backtesting
- Paper Trading

Real-money trading is intentionally outside the initial scope.
